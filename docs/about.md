# About ts-obom

***ts-obom*** grew out of threat-modelling work: to draw trust boundaries around a system you need to know which identities the software runs as and what those identities may touch. Dependency scans do not carry that information; the infrastructure definitions do. The extraction was first prototyped inside [ts-scan](https://github.com/trustsource/ts-scan) and then moved into its own tool, because an access graph is a different artefact than a Software Bill of Materials and deserves its own, unambiguous command. Here it is: [ts-obom](https://github.com/trustsource/ts-obom).
